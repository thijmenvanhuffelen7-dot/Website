Frequently asked questions

Question not listed? Get in touch.

Do you work in my own accounts?+

Yes. I build everything in your accounts, such as Klaviyo and Shopify. So everything stays yours, even if we stop working together.

Which tasks can you automate?+

Almost anything that happens the same way every day: customer questions, returns, order updates, inventory alerts, invoices, product copy and reports. In the free audit we look at what takes you the most time.

I don't have Klaviyo yet. Can you set it up?+

Yes. I set up the account, connect it to your store and move over your existing subscribers.

Can an AI agent make mistakes with my customers?+

An agent is tested on real questions before it goes live and only takes actions we agree on upfront. Anything uncertain always goes to you.

How long until everything is live?+

That depends on what we build. After the free audit you get a plan with a fixed delivery date.

Am I tied to a subscription?+

No. You pay for a build once. Monthly support is optional.
