Frequently asked questions

Question not listed? Get in touch.

What does working with Scalvio cost?+

That depends on your situation: which platforms, how many creatives and how big your ad budget is. We discuss it in the free strategy call, so you get a proposal that fits.

How much ad budget do I need?+

There is no fixed amount that works for everyone. You need enough budget to test properly; how much depends on your product, your margin and your goals. In the call you get honest advice, even if it's not yet a good moment to start.

How quickly will I see results?+

I can't guarantee results. The first weeks are about testing and learning what works for your brand. From the start you get a weekly overview of the numbers, so you see exactly how it's going.

Which brands do you work with?+

Online stores and brands that want to grow with Meta and TikTok ads. In the free call we look at whether we're a good fit. If not, I'll tell you honestly.

Do I need to supply content?+

No. I can make creatives and UGC for you. Already have photos or videos? Then I'm happy to use them.

Am I tied to a long contract?+

Monthly services can be cancelled each month, unless your quote says otherwise. And you always stay the owner of your ad account and data.
